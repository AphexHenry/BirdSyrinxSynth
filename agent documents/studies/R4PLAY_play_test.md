# R4PLAY — playing the new Syrinx window (WP-R4, criterion 9)

> Written **before** the human plays it, like the studies' pre-registered rules. Results go in §4.

## 1. Question

Can a musician play Syrinx's new Play page from a MIDI keyboard without help? Do the live display and the presets help? R4 does not change the sound (the default preset equals R1 sample for sample), so this is a **feel** test, not a blind listening test.

## 2. Setup (about 15 minutes)

- Open the Standalone app: `build/release/plugin/Syrinx_artefacts/Release/Standalone/Syrinx.app`.
- If your MIDI keyboard does not play, open **Options > Audio/MIDI Settings** and tick it. Syrinx tries to switch MIDI inputs on by itself the first time.
- The computer keyboard and the on-screen keys also play.

## 3. Tasks and questions

**Find each of these without help.** Note any you had to look for.
- a. Every syllable lands on your note (*Snap to note*, or the mod wheel).
- b. The bird sings a tune you play (*Follow keys*).
- c. A bigger, lower bird (*Size*).
- d. Longer pauses between phrases (*Pause*).
- e. Three birds at once, spread wide (*Birds* ≥ 3, *Spread*).
- f. No room (*Room*).
- g. Save your own preset, switch to another one, then find yours again.
- h. *(added with D-030, before the test)* Hold a key for 1–2 s and let go: the bird ends with its long rising call. Short presses (under 0.6 s) must never bring it.

**Questions:**
1. The display: does it help you see what the bird does? Answer *helps*, *neutral* or *confusing*, and say what is confusing.
2. Presets: go through all 10 with the arrows. Which do you like most and least? Any to remove or add?
3. Look: what feels right or wrong (colours, sizes, words)?
4. Playing: anything unexpected? For example glitches when switching presets while playing, stuck notes, lag between key and sound.

### Decision rule (fixed before the test)

R4 is accepted when all three hold:
- **(A)** Tasks a–g can be done. Anything that needed help becomes a fix in a feedback round.
- **(B)** There is no audible glitch when switching presets while playing, and task h behaves as described.
- **(C)** The display is rated *helps* or *neutral*. *Confusing* means it gets fixed before approval.

Colour, layout and wording requests are fixed in a feedback round. They do not block (A)–(C).
