What note is this bird syllable?

PITCH1 · pitch matching · about 20 minutes · progress is saved, you can pause any time

  1. Use headphones or good monitors. Set a comfortable level with the reference tone and do not change it during the test.
  2. Each trial plays a short bird syllable, then a steady tone.
  3. Move the slider until the steady tone has the same pitch as the syllable: the single note that best represents it. Don't try to follow the movement; ask yourself "which note is it?".
  4. Tell how clear the syllable's pitch was. If there is no clear note, still give your best match and choose "no clear note".
  5. Replay as often as you like. Loop mode repeats bird → tone while you adjust.

The first 2 trials are practice. The slider's ends and starting point change on every trial, so the middle of the slider means nothing.
