Does the bird sing around this note?

PITCH2 · about 15 minutes · progress is saved, you can pause any time

  1. Each trial plays a soft held note (around the middle of a keyboard), then the bird sings over it, at its own height, a few octaves up.
  2. Answer one question: does the bird sing around this note? Not "exactly in tune": does it belong to that note, dancing around it, or is it somewhere else?
  3. Sometimes you hear a whole phrase, sometimes a single syllable. Judge each on its own.
  4. Answer quickly, by feel. Replay if you need to. The next trial starts by itself.

Headphones, same volume throughout. The first 4 trials are practice.
