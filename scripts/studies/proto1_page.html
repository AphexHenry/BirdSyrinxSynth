Bird keyboard: prototype 1 (A + B)

Tap a key: the bird sings one real syllable (a random one) on that note. Hold the key and it keeps singing around the note. Release it and the bird finishes the syllable, then stops.

MIDI: checking… Computer keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K (US layout positions) · Z / X octave · Space sustain

Voice

Bird harmonics: 2nd and 3rd harmonics at the level measured in your recording (−30 / −33 dB), no breath (COLOUR1 + COLOUR2).

A syllable's note is…

Melody

naturalall on the note

The first syllable can land exactly on your note. The following ones (while you hold) are placed by the slider, snap . Natural: the bird keeps its own melody around your note. Mod wheel = slider.

What the bird sings

pause

Pause between phrases while you hold the key.

Height

−12+12

Own height: each key sings its note name at the bird's natural height (the octave you play doesn't matter). Follow the keyboard: the bird sings 3 octaves above your key, so melodies keep their shape (best between about C4 and C6). Size semitones; negative = a bigger, lower bird.
