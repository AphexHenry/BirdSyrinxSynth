Length and pitch range

  1. Length stretches or squeezes a syllable in time (60 % = shorter, 200 % = twice as long). Its pitches stay the same.
  2. Pitch range squeezes or widens how far the pitch moves around the syllable's note (60 % = flatter, 200 % = twice as wide a sweep). The note stays where it is.
  3. Each button plays that version; ▶ all plays them one after the other; ▶ random plays five random draws of both (anywhere in 60–200 %, shorter and longer equally likely), as the plugin would.
  4. Below, two real phrases: sung as recorded, then with each syllable at a random length and/or range. New draw gives other random values.
